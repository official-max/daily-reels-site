About this app

A personal tool, not a public service

daily-reels is used only by its owner. The owner signs in once with their own Google account so the tool can upload videos to their own YouTube channel. It does not ask anyone else to sign in and does not collect anyone's data. Read the Privacy Policy to see what Google data is used and why.
